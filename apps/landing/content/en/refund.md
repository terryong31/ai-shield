## One-time pass, no auto-renewal

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info"}
**In short:** you pay once. There is no subscription, so you never need to cancel.
::

The ai-shield 30-Day Pass ($5 USD) is a one-time payment processed by Stripe. It does not auto-renew, so no subscription cancellation is needed to prevent future charges.

## Statutory-minimum refund policy

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info"}
**In short:** no change-of-mind refunds after the pass is active, but a full refund when something went wrong.
::

Because digital pass access activates immediately upon payment, change-of-mind refunds are not offered once the pass is active. However, in accordance with Malaysia's Consumer Protection Act 1999 (CPA 1999) and mandatory local consumer laws, we provide a full refund or prompt remedy if:

- your pass fails to activate after payment,
- you were billed twice in error, or
- the paid service suffers a major unresolved defect.

## How to request a refund

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info"}
**In short:** email us with your receipt. We answer within 3 business days.
::

Email [support@ai-shield.my](mailto:support@ai-shield.my) with your account email and Stripe receipt or payment date. We respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method via Stripe and typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days.
